About Me


Hello! My name is Lars, and I’m from the Frisian region of the Netherlands. I study CMGT at Saxion University of Applied Sciences in Enschede, where I currently take on the role of a second-year E/A (Engineer & Artist).

My passion for making games started back in 2017, as shown in the timeline below. Since then, I’ve been deeply driven to create projects that truly mean something, whether that meaning is through invoking emotions using games telling beautiful stories or building experiences that can genuinely help people in real life.

I’m a very passionate developer, especially when working on projects that aim to give something back to the player. I believe every game should have a soul behind it and should lend a open ear to them who play it.

While a game may technically just be pixels on a screen, it can create experiences even our own minds sometimes cant comprehend.
